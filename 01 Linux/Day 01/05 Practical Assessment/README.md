# Day 01 practical assessment

![Independent assessment checkpoints](../07%20Images/practical-assessment.svg)

Text equivalent: Locate your saved baseline, verify your observations, create your independent record, and explain the limits. Passing requires actual evidence.

Complete this after the guided lab. You may consult the notes or official documentation for syntax. Do not copy another learner's observations or use a fabricated screenshot.

## Your task

Start from a directory outside your Day 01 workspace. Return to your workspace and read your baseline without re-creating it.

Use vi to create a separate `assessment.md` in your private Day 01 directory. Include:

- Your assessment timestamp with timezone offset.
- Your distribution, kernel name, architecture, and numeric user ID, based on actual observations.
- The absolute location of the baseline.
- One explanation of why kernel identity and distribution identity are different questions.
- One explanation of what the disk-space check covers and what it leaves unknown.
- One fact you verified by reopening the baseline.
- Your next learning checkpoint.

Save the file and independently verify its contents. Keep personal account details private. If the file already exists from an earlier attempt, preserve it and use a new attempt filename.

## Pass criteria

| Criterion | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Safe context | Your lab account has a nonzero numeric ID and you are in your private workspace. |
| Location | You find the existing baseline from outside its directory and state its absolute path correctly. |
| Accurate record | Your recorded environment values match actual command output. |
| Saved result | You read both files from the shell after leaving the editor. |
| Reasoning | You distinguish kernel, distribution, filesystem space, and application health without unsupported claims. |
| Continuity | Your next action identifies a concrete skill to practice or a gap to resolve. |

All criteria must be met for completion. A failure is a checkpoint to repair, not a reason to restart the course. Use [troubleshooting](../06%20Troubleshooting/README.md) and the [evidence checklist](../08%20Evidence/README.md).

Use the pass criteria to evaluate your actual results. If a criterion is not met, resolve that specific gap and repeat the relevant check before continuing.
