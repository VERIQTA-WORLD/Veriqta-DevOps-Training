# Linux, Day 01

## Your environment and first system baseline

Before you install an application, you need to identify where it will run, which account you are using, and where its files belong. Today you inspect your Linux environment and save a starting record you can verify and reopen.

Your outcome is a real saved baseline, not a deployed service. Allow around 45 to 60 minutes; environment setup can take longer.

## Before you start

Use an authorized Linux lab, a regular user account, Bash, common Linux utilities, and vi. The notes explain these requirements. You need no cloud account, paid service, installation command, or administrator access for this lesson.

During independent study, compare each command's actual result with its expected pattern before proceeding. If a command fails, use the troubleshooting section rather than skipping verification.

## Your completion check

You can identify the kernel, distribution, architecture, current account, and workspace. You can read your saved baseline after leaving and returning to its directory. You can explain what your checks establish and what they do not prove. Complete the projects, knowledge checks, and independent assessment before moving on.

## Evidence
