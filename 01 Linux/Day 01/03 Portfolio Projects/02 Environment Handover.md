# Project 02, Your Environment Handover

## The problem

Another learner should be able to find and interpret your baseline without guessing which machine, directory, or observation it describes.

## Your deliverable

In your private Day 01 workspace, use vi to create `handover.md`. Write a short handover containing:

- The scope, a Linux training environment, not a production system.
- The absolute location and capture time of your baseline.
- The commands you would use to navigate to it and read it.
- One reason distribution identity and machine architecture matter before installing software.
- One fact that remains unknown, such as the future application's health.
- The next action, finish Day 01 checks and wait for the next published Linux day.

Use actual observations and your own explanation. If the file already exists, preserve the previous attempt and choose a new filename.

## Verify the project

Read your saved handover from the shell. Start outside your Day 01 directory and use your written navigation instructions to find the baseline. Confirm that the instructions work and the handover does not expose information you should keep private.

You pass when another authorized reader could locate the artifact and understand both the facts and their limits. This task practices operational communication, not a deployed infrastructure service.
