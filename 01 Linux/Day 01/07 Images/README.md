# Day 01 images

These are exact teaching diagrams. They are not screenshots or proof that a learner ran the commands. Every diagram has a text explanation in its learning section.

| Image | Teaching purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Environment map](environment-map.svg) | Separate terminal, shell, kernel, distribution, identity, and workspace. |
| [Baseline checkpoints](baseline-checkpoints.svg) | Inspect, record, reopen, and explain your saved result. |
| [Portfolio projects](portfolio-projects.svg) | Connect your baseline to an environment handover. |
| [Knowledge checks](knowledge-checks.svg) | Predict, inspect, and explain before reading answers. |
| [Practical assessment](practical-assessment.svg) | Check the independent task's observable outcomes. |
| [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.svg) | Diagnose from evidence and verify recovery. |
| [Evidence](evidence.svg) | Distinguish real observations, expected examples, and safe public copies. |

The diagrams use a 1080 by 1350 portrait canvas and Arial styling. Actual terminal screenshots belong to your private evidence until reviewed and redacted. No replacement official logo has been invented.
