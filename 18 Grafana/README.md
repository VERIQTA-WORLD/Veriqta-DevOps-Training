# 18 Grafana

Explain user impact using useful dashboards and signals.

Build your skills through practical lessons, hands-on labs, portfolio projects, and independent assessments. The curriculum connects technical understanding with verifiable results and troubleshooting practice.

## Your daily curriculum

| Day | Lesson | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 01 | Data sources and first dashboard | Connect metrics and display meaningful service behavior. |
| Day 02 | User journeys, SLIs, and SLOs | Define a measurable reliability target and its limits. |
| Day 03 | Logs, traces, and correlation | Follow evidence across complementary signals. |
| Day 04 | Alerts and ownership | Reduce ambiguity in a failure notification. |
| Day 05 | Observability portfolio assessment | Explain a controlled incident from the user-facing signals. |

Your portfolio connects working artifacts with evidence, explanations, and limitations. Document the decisions you make, the failures you investigate, and the checks that verify your results.
