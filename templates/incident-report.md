# Incident report template

Use this for an authorized lab incident or a real incident you are allowed to document. State which it is. Use synthetic identifiers in public versions.

## Impact

What failed, who or what was affected, and what evidence supports that assessment?

## Timeline

Record timestamps with timezone, observations, actions, and results. Separate facts from hypotheses.

## Investigation

What competing explanations did you consider? What evidence supported or ruled out each one?

## Recovery

What changed, why was it safe to try, and how did you verify recovery from the user's perspective?

## Causes and contributing conditions

Explain the failure chain without blaming individuals or calling every incident a single root cause.

## Follow-up

List specific prevention, detection, and recovery improvements, their owners if known, and how they can be verified. Do not invent dates or owners.
