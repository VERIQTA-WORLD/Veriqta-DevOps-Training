## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, expected load, cost boundaries, security needs, and assumptions.

## Options

Compare feasible choices and identify what evidence is available or missing.

## Decision

Explain your selected option and the tradeoffs you accepted.

## Consequences

Describe operational responsibilities, failure modes, costs, and limits.

## Verification and review

State how you will verify the choice and what change would justify revisiting it.
