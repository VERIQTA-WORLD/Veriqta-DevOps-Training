# Runbook template

## Purpose and scope

State the symptom or operation this procedure addresses, the environment, and who is authorized to run it.

## Prerequisites and risk

List required access, tools, backups, likely side effects, and conditions that require stopping or escalation.

## Diagnosis

Give ordered observations and explain how their results affect the next step. Preserve relevant evidence before changes.

## Action

Provide exact, reviewed commands and clearly marked placeholders. Explain meaningful side effects before execution.

## Verification

Show how to verify recovery, including user-visible behavior and relevant signals.

## Rollback and cleanup

Describe the recovery path if the change fails and identify remaining resources or costs.

## References

Record the tested environment, source revision, review date, and official documentation.
