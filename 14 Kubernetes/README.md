# 14 Kubernetes

Deploy, operate, and troubleshoot the multi-tier application.

Build your skills through practical lessons, hands-on labs, portfolio projects, and independent assessments. The curriculum connects technical understanding with verifiable results and troubleshooting practice.

## Your daily curriculum

| Day | Lesson | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 01 | Cluster model and local setup | Identify control-plane and workload responsibilities in a local cluster. |
| Day 02 | API, manifests, and reconciliation | Apply desired state and inspect the resulting objects. |
| Day 03 | Pods and runtime evidence | Inspect a running workload and diagnose a startup failure. |
| Day 04 | Deployments and rollout | Change a workload version and verify its rollout. |
| Day 05 | Services and discovery | Route requests to healthy application endpoints. |
| Day 06 | Configuration and secrets | Provide configuration with explicit secret-handling limits. |
| Day 07 | Probes and resource controls | Distinguish startup, readiness, liveness, requests, and limits. |
| Day 08 | Storage and persistent data | Verify data through workload replacement. |
| Day 09 | Scheduling and placement | Diagnose an unscheduled workload from evidence. |
| Day 10 | Traffic entry and TLS | Expose the application through a supported lab traffic-entry option. |
| Day 11 | RBAC and workload access | Restrict and test the intended permissions. |
| Day 12 | Network policy | Prove permitted and denied application connections. |
| Day 13 | Scaling and capacity | Observe a controlled load change and evaluate scaling behavior. |
| Day 14 | Upgrade and recovery planning | Explain compatibility, disruption, backups, and recovery boundaries. |
| Day 15 | Multi-symptom cluster incident | Diagnose a controlled failure chain without random restarts. |
| Day 16 | Kubernetes portfolio assessment | Deliver the application with traffic, storage, monitoring hooks, and recovery evidence. |

Your portfolio connects working artifacts with evidence, explanations, and limitations. Document the decisions you make, the failures you investigate, and the checks that verify your results.
