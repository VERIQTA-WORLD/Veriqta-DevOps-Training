# 17 Prometheus

Measure service behavior and detect a meaningful failure.

Build your skills through practical lessons, hands-on labs, portfolio projects, and independent assessments. The curriculum connects technical understanding with verifiable results and troubleshooting practice.

## Your daily curriculum

| Day | Lesson | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 01 | Metrics and instrumentation | Expose a small set of useful application metrics. |
| Day 02 | Targets, labels, and cardinality | Scrape intended targets without uncontrolled label growth. |
| Day 03 | PromQL and recording rules | Answer operational questions from metric data. |
| Day 04 | Alert rules and routing | Trigger and route an actionable lab alert. |
| Day 05 | Retention and observability assessment | Explain storage limits and detect a controlled service failure. |

Your portfolio connects working artifacts with evidence, explanations, and limitations. Document the decisions you make, the failures you investigate, and the checks that verify your results.
