# 08 JFrog Artifactory

Publish and retrieve a controlled versioned artifact.

Build your skills through practical lessons, hands-on labs, portfolio projects, and independent assessments. The curriculum connects technical understanding with verifiable results and troubleshooting practice.

## Your daily curriculum

| Day | Lesson | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 01 | Repositories and access | Explain local, remote, and virtual repositories in a lab. |
| Day 02 | Maven publishing and retrieval | Publish your Java artifact using protected credentials. |
| Day 03 | Build metadata and promotion | Trace an artifact and promote the intended version. |
| Day 04 | Retention and artifact assessment | Apply a bounded retention exercise and demonstrate reproducible retrieval. |

Your portfolio connects working artifacts with evidence, explanations, and limitations. Document the decisions you make, the failures you investigate, and the checks that verify your results.
