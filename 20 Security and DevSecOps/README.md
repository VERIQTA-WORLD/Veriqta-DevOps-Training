# 20 Security and DevSecOps

Review and harden the complete platform with evidence.

Build your skills through practical lessons, hands-on labs, portfolio projects, and independent assessments. The curriculum connects technical understanding with verifiable results and troubleshooting practice.

## Your daily curriculum

| Day | Lesson | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 01 | Threat model and trust boundaries | Map assets, identities, entry points, and credible threats. |
| Day 02 | Identity and secrets review | Reduce access and test the effect of a credential rotation. |
| Day 03 | Dependency and image findings | Triage vulnerabilities using context and remediation evidence. |
| Day 04 | SBOMs, signing, and provenance | Record and verify software origin and composition. |
| Day 05 | Policy and hardening | Apply and test a bounded host or workload control. |
| Day 06 | Audit evidence and incident response | Investigate a synthetic security event without destroying evidence. |
| Day 07 | Backup and disaster recovery | Restore synthetic application data and measure recovery limits. |
| Day 08 | Performance, capacity, and cost review | Evaluate a bounded workload and explain platform tradeoffs. |
| Day 09 | Final platform portfolio | Deliver architecture decisions, runbooks, release evidence, and incident reports. |
| Day 10 | Independent engineering assessment | Defend, operate, troubleshoot, and improve the platform from unfamiliar scenarios. |

Your portfolio connects working artifacts with evidence, explanations, and limitations. Document the decisions you make, the failures you investigate, and the checks that verify your results.
