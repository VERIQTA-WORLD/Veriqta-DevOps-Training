# 04 Bash and Shell Scripting

Build safe and explainable operational automation.

Build your skills through practical lessons, hands-on labs, portfolio projects, and independent assessments. The curriculum connects technical understanding with verifiable results and troubleshooting practice.

## Your daily curriculum

| Day | Lesson | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 01 | Scripts, arguments, and quoting | Create an explained script with safe path handling. |
| Day 02 | Conditions and exit status | Make a health check distinguish success from failure. |
| Day 03 | Loops and functions | Process several synthetic service records predictably. |
| Day 04 | Pipes and structured input | Validate inputs and avoid fragile output parsing. |
| Day 05 | Errors, cleanup, and signals | Handle expected failures and clean up a bounded temporary resource. |
| Day 06 | Operational tooling and linting | Check and improve your service investigation script. |
| Day 07 | Automation portfolio and assessment | Demonstrate a repeatable diagnostic and backup task with failure evidence. |

Your portfolio connects working artifacts with evidence, explanations, and limitations. Document the decisions you make, the failures you investigate, and the checks that verify your results.
