# 19 Argo CD and GitOps

Deliver reviewed desired state and recover from drift.

Build your skills through practical lessons, hands-on labs, portfolio projects, and independent assessments. The curriculum connects technical understanding with verifiable results and troubleshooting practice.

## Your daily curriculum

| Day | Lesson | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 01 | Desired state and reconciliation | Connect reviewed configuration to a lab application. |
| Day 02 | Application definitions and health | Inspect sync and health as different signals. |
| Day 03 | Environment promotion and secrets | Promote an intended version without committing live credentials. |
| Day 04 | Drift, failed sync, and recovery | Diagnose and resolve a controlled delivery problem. |
| Day 05 | GitOps portfolio assessment | Trace a reviewed change through deployment and recovery. |

Your portfolio connects working artifacts with evidence, explanations, and limitations. Document the decisions you make, the failures you investigate, and the checks that verify your results.
