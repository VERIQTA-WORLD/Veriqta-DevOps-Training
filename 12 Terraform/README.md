# 12 Terraform

Review, create, change, and recover infrastructure through code.

Build your skills through practical lessons, hands-on labs, portfolio projects, and independent assessments. The curriculum connects technical understanding with verifiable results and troubleshooting practice.

## Your daily curriculum

| Day | Lesson | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 01 | Providers and first resources | Create a small bounded resource after reviewing its plan. |
| Day 02 | Variables, outputs, and dependencies | Make the lab configuration understandable and reusable. |
| Day 03 | State and sensitive data | Inspect state responsibilities without publishing sensitive values. |
| Day 04 | Remote backend and locking | Configure a suitable backend and verify concurrency protection. |
| Day 05 | Modules and environments | Reuse a small module with explicit environment boundaries. |
| Day 06 | Plans, changes, and drift | Investigate a deliberate difference between code and infrastructure. |
| Day 07 | Import and recovery | Bring a bounded resource under management and explain the risks. |
| Day 08 | Infrastructure portfolio assessment | Rebuild the lab and verify teardown and remaining costs. |

Your portfolio connects working artifacts with evidence, explanations, and limitations. Document the decisions you make, the failures you investigate, and the checks that verify your results.
