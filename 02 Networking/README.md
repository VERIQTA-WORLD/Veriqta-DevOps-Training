# 02 Networking

Trace and diagnose an application request across a network.

Build your skills through practical lessons, hands-on labs, portfolio projects, and independent assessments. The curriculum connects technical understanding with verifiable results and troubleshooting practice.

## Your daily curriculum

| Day | Lesson | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 01 | IP addresses, interfaces, and subnets | Map a small lab network and explain local versus remote destinations. |
| Day 02 | Routes and gateways | Trace how a packet leaves a host and diagnose a controlled route error. |
| Day 03 | Ports, TCP, UDP, and listeners | Match a client connection to a listening application. |
| Day 04 | DNS resolution | Investigate a name-resolution failure using observable results. |
| Day 05 | HTTP, HTTPS, and TLS | Inspect a request, response, and certificate without treating them as one layer. |
| Day 06 | Firewalls and NAT | Explain allowed traffic and diagnose a restricted connection. |
| Day 07 | Proxies and load balancing | Follow a request through a small reverse-proxy lab. |
| Day 08 | Packet evidence and network incident | Use a bounded capture and deliver a request-path troubleshooting portfolio task. |

Your portfolio connects working artifacts with evidence, explanations, and limitations. Document the decisions you make, the failures you investigate, and the checks that verify your results.
