# 13 Ansible

Configure the lab hosts repeatably and safely.

Build your skills through practical lessons, hands-on labs, portfolio projects, and independent assessments. The curriculum connects technical understanding with verifiable results and troubleshooting practice.

## Your daily curriculum

| Day | Lesson | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 01 | Inventory and connectivity | Target only authorized lab hosts. |
| Day 02 | Modules, playbooks, and variables | Apply and explain a small desired configuration. |
| Day 03 | Templates and handlers | Update service configuration and restart only when needed. |
| Day 04 | Roles and secret boundaries | Organize configuration while protecting sensitive values. |
| Day 05 | Idempotence and controlled rollout | Verify repeat execution and diagnose a failed host. |
| Day 06 | Configuration portfolio assessment | Recreate service configuration with a documented recovery path. |

Your portfolio connects working artifacts with evidence, explanations, and limitations. Document the decisions you make, the failures you investigate, and the checks that verify your results.
